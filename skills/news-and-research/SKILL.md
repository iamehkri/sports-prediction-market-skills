# News and Research

## Purpose

Process breaking sports information and determine whether it changes fair probability.

## Research Priorities

Prefer:

1. league source
2. team source
3. official injury / transaction report
4. player or representative
5. credible beat reporter
6. established sports-data provider
7. major sports media
8. aggregator
9. anonymous source

## Breaking-News Workflow

When new information appears:

### 1. Verify

Determine:

- original source
- publication time
- whether confirmed
- whether multiple credible sources agree

### 2. Determine Relevance

Ask:

- Does this change player availability?
- Does this change expected minutes/snaps?
- Does this change tactics?
- Does this change weather assumptions?
- Does this change tournament structure?
- Does this affect settlement?

### 3. Quantify Impact

Estimate:

```text
Previous probability:
New probability:
Probability change:
```

### 4. Check Market Reaction

Determine:

```text
Price before news:
Price after news:
Sportsbook movement:
Prediction-market movement:
```

### 5. Determine Information Edge

The fact that news exists does not create edge.

Edge exists only if the market has not appropriately incorporated the information.

## Rumors

Label unconfirmed information clearly.

Reduce confidence.

Never state rumor as fact.

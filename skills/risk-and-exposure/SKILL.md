# Risk and Exposure

## Purpose

Evaluate analytical risk when discussing multiple positions or correlated markets.

This skill does not determine a person's personal financial suitability.

## Types of Risk

Consider:

- model risk
- information risk
- liquidity risk
- execution risk
- settlement risk
- correlation risk
- event risk
- platform risk

## Correlated Exposure

Positions may look independent while depending on the same underlying event.

Examples:

```text
Team wins game
Team wins division
QB wins MVP
Team wins championship
```

These positions share common drivers.

Do not treat them as four independent bets.

## Portfolio View

For multiple markets, summarize:

```text
MARKET
DIRECTION
FAIR VALUE
PRICE
EDGE
CONFIDENCE
CORRELATION GROUP
MAJOR RISK
```

## Opportunity Score

When useful:

```text
Opportunity Score =
Edge
× Confidence
× Information Quality
× Market Quality
```

Treat this as a ranking heuristic, not an exact law.

A 6-point high-confidence edge in a liquid market can be superior to a 14-point low-confidence discrepancy in a thin market.

## Risk Rule

Analytical conviction and appropriate position size are separate questions.
